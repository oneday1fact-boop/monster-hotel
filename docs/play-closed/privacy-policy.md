# Monster Hotel Privacy Policy / 怪物旅館私隱政策

Effective date / 生效日期: 7 November 2026

Developer / 開發者: Oneday1fact  
Contact / 聯絡: oneday1fact@gmail.com  
Android package: com.oneday1fact.monster_hotel

Monster Hotel is an offline puzzle game. No account is required. Game progress, earned coins, room unlocks, illustrated photographs, decorations and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded to developer servers. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase saves. Android backup may preserve app data according to your device and Google account settings.

This version contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, no real-money purchases, and does not access location, camera, microphone or contacts. The photograph feature records only illustrated game characters and does not use a camera or access your personal photos. Sound and optional haptics use device capabilities.

The developer does not collect or share personal data through the game. Google Play and Android may process account, installation, security, backup and diagnostic information under Google's policies and your settings. If you email support, we receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact the email above. We may retain correspondence where needed to resolve ongoing requests or meet legal obligations.

Delete local game data through Android app settings or by uninstalling. This policy will be updated if future releases introduce new data processing. The game has no user chat or user-generated content. These privacy practices apply to all players.
